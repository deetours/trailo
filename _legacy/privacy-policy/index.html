Privacy & Data Trust Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2026 | Governing OS: Girivah Technologies

At Trailo (an autonomous adventure travel operating layer under Girivah Technologies), our core thesis is building India's trust-first adventure ecosystem. Every design decision, line of code, and data interaction is engineered to reduce doubt, preserve personal security, and guarantee operational safety in isolated alpine environments.

🔒 Core Privacy Commitment: We never sell, lease, or monetize your personal travel preferences, geographical logs, or location telemetry to advertising networks or external data brokers.

1. Information We Collect

To operate our Agentic Intelligence Layer and ensure accurate trip synthesis, Trailo collects the following limited data tiers:

  • Adventure Profile & Physical Fit Data: Self-reported fitness ratings, altitude experience, dietary restrictions, and emergency medical contacts used solely by our AI fit assistant to recommend safe itineraries.
  • Location & Telemetry Logs: Real-time GPS waypoint telemetry, oxygen monitoring integration (when paired with wearable hardwares), and route tracking—strictly activated during confirmed departures for wilderness safety and rescue escalation.
  • Communication & Booking Records: Enquiry workflows, WhatsApp automation interactions, and operator handoff protocols routed through Girivah Partner OS.

2. How We Utilize Your Data

Your data functions exclusively as input for our operational routing and safety infrastructure:

  • Autonomous Itinerary Matchmaking: Computing optimal trails, road trip waypoints, and stays based on real-time climate and environmental risk scores.
  • Emergency SOS & Rescue Escalation: In high-altitude or isolated trekking scenarios, location telemetry is monitored by our Agentic Core to alert local emergency responder grids and Girivah support teams if unexpected deviation or immobility is detected.
  • Operator Coordination: Transmitting necessary logistical manifests (such as sleeping bag ratings required, food preferences, and arrival timings) to verified local guides, drivers, and homestays.

3. Data Governance & Girivah Partner OS

Trailo is tightly integrated into the broader Girivah Operating System. When you book or enquire about a trip, trail, or trek, relevant operational data is securely bridged to Layer 2 (Partner OS) and Layer 3 (Admin Control Center).

Verified local partners (trek guides, homestay owners, expedition drivers) receive only the time-sensitive logistical details required to fulfill your journey. Partner access is governed by strict contractual trust covenants and automated revocation once the departure ends.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You retain sovereign control over your identity within our ecosystem:

  • Right to Erasure: You can request immediate permanent deletion of your Trailo profile, prior trip histories, and fit telemetry at any time by contacting our governance desk.
  • Anonymization of Route Telemetry: After trip completion, personal GPS coordinates are disconnected from your identity and anonymized into aggregated environmental difficulty graphs to improve trail safety for future trekkers.

5. Contact & Privacy Support

For privacy inquiries, security reports, or exercising your data rights under Applicable Data Protection Regulations (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), reach out directly to our architecture ops team:

Email: privacy@girivah.com
